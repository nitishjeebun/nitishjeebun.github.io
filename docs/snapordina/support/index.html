SnapOrdina

Support

SnapOrdina is a fully on-device screenshot organizer for iPhone and iPad.

Photos access

The app needs Photos access to organize screenshots. You can grant full access, choose specific items, or explore the sample library first.

Scanning

Scanning runs while the app is open. Pause and resume from Library or Settings if you need to stop.

Apple Intelligence

Sorting and recategorization require iOS 26 or later, a supported device, and Apple Intelligence enabled. Text recognition, collections, and keyword or date search work without it.

Spotlight

Indexing is optional and off by default. Enable it in Settings only if you want recognized screenshot text to appear in system search.
