Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

QRArtify generates, styles, saves, and shares QR codes on your device. Generation uses on-device frameworks. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Saved QR codes and preview images stay in the app’s local store. They leave the device only when you share them, save them to Photos, or otherwise export them. A share extension can receive contacts, URLs, or vCard files that you choose to send into the app. Contacts and Photos access are used only for those features and only after you grant permission.

QRArtify does not send QR contents, contact details, Wi‑Fi credentials, or artwork to a developer-operated server. If you encode a Wi‑Fi password or personal contact, treat the resulting code as sensitive. Anyone who can scan it can read that payload.

Deleting the app removes its local library. Photos originals you saved yourself remain in Photos.
