QRArtify

Support

QRArtify is an on-device QR design tool for iPhone and iPad. Get it on the App Store.

A styled code will not scan

Raise error correction, increase contrast, and keep a quiet margin. Classic black on white remains the most compatible.

Share extension missing

After install, restart the device once so Contacts and Safari can show QRArtify in the share sheet.

Where codes are stored

The library is local. There is no cloud account. iCloud Backup may include app data if the system backs up the device.
